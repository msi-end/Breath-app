## Full description (max 4000)

### English

Take a calm breath — BreatheCare helps you relax, focus and unwind in just a few minutes a day.

BreatheCare is a gentle, nature-inspired wellness app with guided breathing exercises, simple yoga, Indian pranayama, calming music and healthy-living tips. It works offline, needs no sign-up, and never shows ads while you breathe.

🌿 GUIDED BREATHING
• 10 techniques: Box Breathing, 4-7-8, Deep Belly, Equal, Resonant, Slow Breathing and more
• Follow a glowing shape — square, triangle or circle — that traces each breath
• Inhale, hold and exhale with a countdown, round counter and progress
• Breath holds are optional and never forced — Gentle Mode removes them
• Optional voice guidance and vibration cues

🆘 EMERGENCY CALM
• One tap from the home screen
• Slow, soothing breathing with calming messages
• Works fully offline and never shows ads

🧘 EXPLORE: YOGA, PRANAYAMA & HEALTHY HABITS
• Yoga asanas explained step by step: Tadasana, Vrikshasana, Bhujangasana, Surya Namaskar and more
• Pranayama: Anulom-Vilom, Bhramari, Ujjayi, Sheetali
• Calm practices: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, body scan, progressive muscle relaxation
• Kitchen wisdom: haldi, tulsi, adrak, amla, saunf and other traditional favourites
• Better lifestyle: morning sunlight, sleep routine, mindful eating, desk breaks
• "Watch on YouTube" opens videos for each pose in one tap

🎧 LISTEN
• Relaxing music for sleep, focus, meditation, yoga and energy
• Piano, bansuri, singing bowls, Indian instrumental and more
• Background playback with lock-screen controls
• Music is saved on your phone after the first play — no second download, plays offline
• Favourites, playlists and recently played

😊 MOOD-BASED BREATHING
Tell us how you feel — anxious, stressed, tired, can't focus — and get breathing exercises that fit.

🏆 HABITS & PROGRESS
• Daily challenges with streaks
• Weekly summary, total minutes and favourite techniques
• Optional morning and evening reminders

🌐 MADE FOR YOU
• English and Hindi
• Light and dark themes
• No account needed — optional backup with a simple User ID and PIN
• Your mood and wellness data are never used for advertising

BreatheCare is a relaxation and wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. If you have a health concern, please talk to a doctor.
